Anthropic Goes Public in Largest IPO Ever
The AI company behind Claude completed its trading debut on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT," raising $81.3 billion in the largest IPO in history. Shares opened at $111.50, up 34% from the $83 IPO price, before closing at $97—giving the five-year-old company a final valuation of $1.3 trillion. The landmark debut makes Anthropic the first pure-play AI lab to go public, though shares fell 5% in after-hours trading amid broader questions about AI valuations. Read morevia CNBC
